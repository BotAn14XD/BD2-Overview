    ¶

    The Burst System is one of the costume upgrading systems.
    It allows you to spend more SP to activate the ability in exchange for having the ability enhanced.

    To access the Burst system, select the desired costume in the Costume tab, press Upgrade in the bottom right, and pick the Burst icon.

    Image Guide

    Burst Menu Image

    Burst Mechanics¶

    There are a total of 3 Bursts per costume, with each subsequent tier requiring the previous one to be upgraded first.

    Effects from all Bursts stack, meaning if you activate Burst 3, you will have all effects from Bursts 1-3. This means that Burst Cost also stacks.

    Upgrade Cost

    • 5★ Costume:
      • Burst 1: 60 Sparks of Rampage & 1.5M Gold
      • Burst 2: 60 Sparks of Rampage & 1.5M Gold
      • Burst 3: 60 Sparks of Rampage & 1.5M Gold
      • Total: 180 Sparks of Rampage & 4.5M Gold

    For each Costume, the SP Cost and Effects are different.

    • To see all the Costumes with Burst, check the Miraheze Wiki.

    Sparks of Rampage

    To upgrade the Bursts, you need the currency called Spark of Rampage. It is obtained from many sources:

    • Event Shop: 30 / season — 60 / month
    • Golden Thread Shop: 55 / month
    • Powder of Hope Shop: 55 / month
    • Refinement Remnants Shop: 200 / month
    • Mirror Wars Shop: 55 / month
    • Guild Shop: 50 / month
    • Golden Colosseum Shop: 55 / month
    • Taros Tactical Manual: 30 / month
    • Total theoretical: 560 / month ~ 3.1 Costumes / month
    • Total realistic: 450 ~ 505 / month ~ 2.5 ~ 2.8 Costumes / month

    Burst in a Battle¶

    If you upgraded your Burst, you can use that in your battle.

    When you choose the skill with upgraded Burst, you can press the little arrow to choose the Burst Tier, after which an animation and screen distortion will be visible for a brief period of time, indicating Burst has been activated.

    Image Guide

    Additionally, active Burst will be displayed in their own Cards.

    Image Demonstration

    Aside from the things mentioned above, the Skill Card will now have a light blue border and extra SP shown in orange, and burst effects will be highlighted.

    Image Demonstration

    When Burst is used directly, special pop-up appears for a brief moment as well.

    Image Demonstration

    Auto Burst¶

    In the Battle, you can activate the Auto Burst button, located at the top right of the screen.

    Auto Burst Button

    When active, Burst will be activated automatically; however, it will take a lower priority compared to Costume activation.

    That means if you do not have enough SP to activate Burst and Costume at the same time, Costume will be activated only.

    The Auto Battle feature enables Auto Burst automatically.

    Unique Game Mode Behaviors¶

    • In Last Night, the maximum possible Burst is applied automatically to the costume whenever the costume was upgraded beforehand.
    • In Golden Colosseum, the maximum possible Burst is used based on your SP.
      • If Rules grant infinite (∞) SP, Burst is applied similar to Last Night. Otherwise, Burst is applied after all costumes have activated their Abilities.

    Burst view in Collection¶

    In the collection, Burst is displayed under the Potential Liberation icon and consists of 3 tongues of flame, corresponding to individual bursts.

    Burst Demonstration in Companion Tab

    As was said above, each separate tongue of flame represents a separate burst, which means you can visually differentiate the different stages of the burst.

    Different Burst progression

    Priority Guide¶

    Since Sparks of Rampage is a limited resource, it is important to focus on the costumes that will benefit you more in the long run.

    General Advice

    • This system is very expensive for a new player. It is better not to heavily invest in it since it is a gold sink.
    • Despite it being a gold sink, you really should accumulate Sparks even if you do not plan on spending them. The scarcity of the resource sets high pressure for a player, and missing Sparks is a bad move.
      • That doesn't really apply to Powder of Hope / Golden Thread shops, because while you can afford to start buying Sparks from the latter, non-costume purchases in the Powder of Hope shop remain mostly end-game territory.
    • The whole Burst System is more designed for Fiend Hunter / Guild Raid than it is for the story. Since the introduction of the Burst system, meta trends show that, similar to Potential Liberation, Supports have higher priority over DPS. That does not mean that DPS should be ignored completely, since there are some good effects from it, but you should be wise when investing in property-specific or situational units like DPS.
    • Each costume has different Burst Effects which makes the whole priority a bit more subjective and situational, so you do not have to follow the list 1 to 1 and should invest based on your own progress instead.
