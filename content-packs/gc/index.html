    ¶

    Golden Colosseum is a weekly seasonal PvP content.

    TL;DR

    • A Golden Colosseum season lasts one week, concluding with a brief Settlement period.
    • Golden Colosseum does not use Gear. Only Costumes upgrade levels (+5), Potential Liberation, Awakening, and Bursts matter.
    • Completing missions and ranking up rewards Aurum Coins, which can be spent in the Golden Colosseum Shop.

    Golden Colosseum Main Menu

    Basics¶

    The Golden Colosseum is unlocked upon clearing all Pagan Tower (Story Pack 6) Main Quests.

    To fight, you set up a team and Blessings tailored to the active season's Rules. While core combat mechanics remain stable across most seasons, specific parameters (like grid size, unit limits, and bans) rotate regularly.

    Similarly to Mirror Wars, the battles are automatic and do not require an active input from a player.

    Winning against the opponent grants Points, and losing decreases Points, correspondingly. Unlike the Mirror Wars, though, losing in Defense does not affect your points; only your offense matters.

    Repeat Battle

    To save your time, you can use Repeat Battle feature. It will automatically start the battles and skip them after first Costume's Action until the set number of attempts is exhausted.

    To activate it, press Repeat Battle at the bottom right corner of the Golden Colosseum Menu.

    Image Guide

    Team¶

    The team consists of Costumes, not Characters. You set up one team for both offense and defense, as well as for going first or second.

    Each Costume is separate, which means you can put multiple Costumes of the same Character. Additionally, each Costume is affected by its own Costume bond, Awakening, Character Level, and Costume Upgrade level. The only thing Costumes are not affected by is Gear.

    To set the team, press the Formation button, then Replace, pick the needed Costume, and, finally, place it on the field and save the changes.

    Image Guide

    Additionally, in the Formation tab, you can freely change the order of your Costumes, similar to the regular battles.

    Blessings¶

    Blessings are effects that impact you or your enemy. They are separated into three categories: Offense, Defense, and Utility.

    To access the Blessings menu, press the Formation button followed by Settings on the right.

    Image Guide

    Blessings Menu

    Each Blessing costs a specific amount of Blessing Points, based on how impactful the Blessing is. You can equip a limited number of Blessings, dictated by the total Blessing Points available. You also have to make two separate Blessing builds for going First or Second, with going second granting extra points to compensate for turn order.

    To check each Blessing, refer to the Blessing Menu mentioned above. Alternatively, check Souseha's Database.

    What Blessings to choose?

    It comes down to the used team and the strategy. Check the Guide section below.

    If you want to use Blessings of the other person but have only a list for going first or second, you can usually adjust it by picking higher/lower potency Blessings for more/less points.

    For example, if the person has Curse of Limits I in their "going first" lineup, you can try picking both Curse of Limits I and Curse of Limits II for when you go second.

    Blessing Order

    Blessing order matters. They activate in order from the top to the bottom. That means, using Grace before Barrier of Life will provide more Energy Guard compared to using Barrier of Life before Grace.

    Rules¶

    Every 2 Seasons, a new set of Rules is released. They dictate how the season goes. There are a total of 17 Rules, with 7 Rules changing (Rotating), while the rest are untouched/consistent (as of now).

    To check the Rules of current and previous Seasons, press the corresponding menu.

    "Consistent" Rules¶

    While parameters labeled as (Consistent) can technically be altered by developers in future seasons, they have remained stable defaults throughout 34 seasons:

    • Weekly Entry: You are given 100 base attempts per week. You can purchase up to 3 refills (100 attempts each) for 10 Dia per refill, capping out at 400 attempts for 30 Dia total per season.
    • Initiative: You cannot choose whether you go first or second; initiative is decided randomly at the start of battle.
    • Action Flow: Units alternate turns individually based on placement order.
      (Player Costume 1 → Enemy Costume 1 → Player Costume 2 → Enemy Costume 2...) (if you go first).
      • If the Enemy Costume 1 dies from Player Costume 1, Enemy Costume 2 takes its place, until all deployed enemy Costumes have acted.
      • If the enemy used all their Costumes in a given Turn, but you still have multiple unused Costumes, they will do their actions without the enemy using skills once again.
    • Cooldowns: Costumes have no Cooldowns between Turns.
    • SP Costs: SP is set to ∞ (ignored/bypassed), meaning skills do not cost SP to cast. However, available SP for scaling mechanics (e.g., Olivier) is treated as baseline 0.
    • Starting Points: You start with 1000 Ranking points.
    • Death Time Turn: At Turn 5, combat enters Death Time. Unlike standard game modes where skills remain active during Death Time, skills in Golden Colosseum are disabled completely, forcing units to use basic attacks alongside the ramping Death Time stat buffs/debuffs.

    Missions¶

    Missions are tasks that you can complete once per season. Completing missions grants you Aurum Coins, which are spent in the Shop.

    To get them, press the corresponding icon in the Golden Colosseum menu.

    If you are very busy, ...

    ...at least win 10 fights per Season. You will lose around 22% of coins from Missions alone and some coins from ranking, but at least it will be better than not participating at all.

    10 wins is not hard if you find a good team for a given season.

    Missions Availability

    You cannot complete Missions during the Settlement Period.

    However, you still can claim rewards during the Settlement Period, given you satisfied the conditions beforehand.

    This is helpful if you complete your battles right before the deadline or forget to claim your rewards before settlement begins.

    Ranking¶

    Similarly to the Mirror Wars, there are 29 Rankings. Reaching a specific Rank awards you with a different amount of Aurum Coins at the end of the Season.

    The Season lasts for a week with a Settlement Period that starts at 15:00 UTC on the last day. It happens on Wednesday (UTC), the last day of the game week. During the Settlement Period, you cannot perform any Attacks, so be sure to use your Attempts before the period.

    Golden Colosseum ranks players strictly by relative percentile brackets across the active player base. At the end of the season, all active players are sorted by their final Rating Points and divided into percentage cutoffs (e.g., Top 1%, Top 5%, Top 10%, etc.).

    Understanding Game Wording

    The game UI uses inverted wording for the ranking system. Here are some examples of how to translate the brackets:

    • Within the Top 0.1%: You performed better than at least 99.9% of players.
    • Above Top 10% - Below Top 12.5%: You performed worse than 10% of players but still better than 87.5%. In other words, you are within the Top 10% — 12.5% bracket of players.

    If you do not care about PvP to the degree you want to actually climb the ladder, copying someone's teams will do. This will give you decent Rank and 40 wins easily to complete all missions.

    For that, you have plenty of options:

    Small advice

    Since Costumes rely on progression, it would make sense to pick the options that include your better upgraded Costumes.

    Search for the one you're comfortable with.

    Shop¶

    Shop is a place where you can spend earned Aurum Coins. To access it, either press the Shop icon directly from the Golden Colosseum Pack or find the Golden Colosseum Shop in a Content Shop section of a regular Shop.

    Image Guide

    Golden Colosseum Shop

    Shop Priority¶

    Assuming you are reaching Platinum II (55% — 50% bracket) and completing all Missions, you will receive 2100 Aurum Coins per season, which translates to 8400 Aurum Coins per 4 weeks (roughly a month).

    1. Property Selective Draw Exchange Ticket x2 — 2000 Aurum Coins
    2. Spark of Rampage x55 — 550 Aurum Coins
    3. Draw Ticket x10 — 2000 Aurum Coins
    4. Refining Crystals x1000 — 2000 Aurum Coins
    5. Ancient Crystals x50 — on leftovers — 1000 Aurum Coins

    Optional (if you have extra Aurum Coins):

    1. Awakening Elixir x100 — 1000 Aurum Coins
    2. Gold x2M — 2000 Aurum Coins
    3. Refining Powder x40K — 1000 Aurum Coins
    • Total without optional: 7550 Aurum Coins
    • Total with optional: 11550 Aurum Coins

    Assuming you are reaching Sapphire I (10% — 12.5% bracket) and completing all Missions, you will receive 2500 Aurum Coins per season, which translates to 10000 Aurum Coins per 4 weeks (roughly a month).

    1. Spark of Rampage x55 — 550 Aurum Coins
    2. Refining Crystals x1000 — 2000 Aurum Coins
    3. Draw Ticket x10 — 2000 Aurum Coins
    4. Gold x2M — 2000 Aurum Coins

    Optional:

    1. Awakening Elixir x100 — if necessary — 1000 Aurum Coins
    2. Ancient Crystals x50 — on leftovers — 1000 Aurum Coins
    3. Property Selective Draw Exchange Ticket x2 — if necessary — 2000 Aurum Coins
    4. Refining Powder x40K — on leftovers — 1000 Aurum Coins
