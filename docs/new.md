## Pack System

Almost all content is divided into **Packs**. To switch the Packs, press the Cartridge in **Home Screen**.

!!! image "Image Guide"

    ![Switching Packs](assets/images/new-player-guide/pack-switch.avif)

!!! warning "A Common Mistake"

    **Story** tab serves for an **Archive**, not progressing down the Story. You can read stuff from there, but it will not grand any progress.

## Teams

Generally speaking, **there is no universal team for every content**. Broadly, it depends on **enemies**.

However, for simplification, in a story you tend to run the same core stuff with some modifications.

The general idea is **1-3 DPS**, with rest being **Supports**. **3 Supports** and **2 DPS** is author's preferred team composition for simple fights, although it can vary.

!!! abstract "Costumes vs Characters"

    To clarify things, in a battle you use **Characters**.
    
    On the image and in the list, **Costumes** are provided as a **Turn 1** option. If you use these Costumes (Skills) on Turn 1, you have more chances to finish the battle in a single turn.
