#

Rapport is a system where you interact with specific Costumes via visual novel-style 'Diner Talks,' unlocking personal character stories and a global account buff that permanently boosts your team's Crit Rate.

!!! tip "TL;DR"

     * You get **3 Talks a day** total (not per Costume) to spend on owned Costumes; picking the **preferred answer** or gifting a **Costume's favorite Drink** gives the most EXP.
     * Leveling a Costume's Rapport unlocks its **Personal Story** and works toward the **Rapport Buff**, a permanent, global {{CritRate}} **Crit Rate**{.white} bonus (up to 50%, reached at 10 maxed Costumes).
     * A Costume's max Level Cap depends on its own upgrade progress: 10 on acquisition, 15 after Awakening, 20 after Potential Liberation — Awakening is mandatory for Level 15 even if Potential Liberation is already done.
     * Jump straight to the [Rapport Answers & Gifts List](#costume-list) if you're searching for one.

!!! image "Rapport Menu"
     ![Rapport Menu](../assets/images/rapport/main-menu.avif)

To access the Rapport Menu, either click on the Rapport button on the Home Screen or press the Talk button in the Companion tab for any Costume with an existing Rapport. Alternatively, use [Glupy Diner](../content-packs/glupy-diner.md) to access the Rapport Feature as well.

## Overview

Each day you are given 3 **Talks** to interact with Costumes. You can interact with one costume only once, which essentially means **you can interact with up to 3 Costumes per day** and your attempts are reset at {{ time('15:00') }} regardless of the number of attempts you had left beforehand.

You can only interact with and gift Costumes **that you currently own**.

Not every Costume has Rapport yet — whether a given Costume gets one is decided individually by the developers. Newly added Costumes will probably have it, while old ones will slowly get their own.

Interaction happens in the form of **Talks**, which are short situations with two choices. One choice is considered "preferred" and gives more **EXP**, while the other is "standard" and therefore gives less **EXP**.

## Talks

Each Talk is a situation where you have to pick an answer.

!!! image "Talk Image"
     ![Talk Image](../assets/images/rapport/choice.avif)

* If you use the "preferred" answer, you will receive 100 EXP.
* If you use the "standard" answer, you will receive only 80 EXP.

There are a total of **15** Talks for each Costume, with each Talk being labeled as a **Visit** and shown in the **Talk Archives**. Pressing the **Talk** button always grants EXP: while any Visits remain unseen, it picks one of them at **random**; once all 15 have been seen, it repeats one instead, still for full EXP. After all 15 Visits are acquired for the first time, you additionally receive 100 {{Dia}} **Dias** as a one-time reward.

The Talk Archives let you review any Visit you've already completed, with its "preferred" / "standard" answers displayed more clearly — this is for reference only and grants no EXP or other reward.

??? image "Answers Display in Archive"
     ![Answers Display in Archive](../assets/images/rapport/answers_cheat.avif)

Upon obtaining the maximum level for the Costume, you can talk an infinite number of times without being limited by the daily 1-Costume restriction, even for Visits you've never seen before — letting you complete the Archive in a matter of minutes.

### Quick Talk

**Quick Talk** is a feature that allows you to skip the Talk but gain only 80 EXP, similar to a "standard" answer. It is available after completing **5 Talks** for a given Costume and behaves like a normal Talk, reducing your daily attempts by 1 for each Quick Talk used.

## Gifts

There are a total of **7** Gifts. You can give them to Costumes to increase their **EXP**. 

All Gifts are obtainable from {{Powder_of_Hope}} **Powder of Hope** and {{Golden_Thread}} **Golden Thread** Shops. 

Each Costume prefers a specific Drink. Gifting this specific drink will grant **40 EXP**, while gifting any other will grant **20** instead.

![Special Drink](../assets/images/rapport/icons/icon_resource190007_401_2.avif "Special Drink"){.icon} **Special Drink** is preferred by **everyone** and will give you **150 EXP** upon gifting.

To fully upgrade the Costume's Rapport, you need 53 Gifts.

!!! example "Better Strategy"
     Instead of gifting 53 Gifts, first **have a single Talk with the best answer**.

     This way, the number of Gifts required is lowered to exactly 50, which makes it easier to progress with multiple Costumes at once (due to the purchase limit of 100 on any given Gift, except ![Special Drink](../assets/images/rapport/icons/icon_resource190007_401_2.avif "Special Drink"){.icon} Special Drink).

     * Early game, **neither** of the shops is good to purchase from.
     * Later, the {{Golden_Thread}} **Golden Thread Shop** is okay to buy from, provided you have bought [**Unidupe**](../misc/slang.md?term=Unidupe), {{Spark_of_Rampage}} **Sparks of Rampage**, {{Tear_of_Goddess}} **Tears of Goddess** and *probably* {{Draw_Ticket}} **Draw Tickets**. This holds until you max your {{CritRate}} **Crit Rate**{.white} bonus.
     * After you obtain the maximum {{CritRate}} **Crit Rate**{.white} bonus, Gifts value drops almost to zero, therefore, there is no reason to buy from either shop anymore.

Each Costume has a separate **Level Cap** that limits the maximum level you can achieve for this costume. Level Cap depends on the Costume / Character upgrade progress.

* Costume Acquired — **Level 10**
* Character Awakening Complete — **Level 15**
* Costume [Potential Liberation](../progression/potentials.md) Complete — **Level 20**

!!! warning "Important Detail"
     You cannot progress further if you have Potential Liberation completed but no Awakening — you must have Awakening to get to Level 15.

     This heavily impacts **Support characters**, who generally do not prioritize or benefit from Awakening as much as your [DPS](../misc/slang.md?term=DPS) units.

Once you obtain Levels 10, 15, and 20, you obtain a special Reward. For Level 10, it is a collection item related to the Costume, while for Levels 15 and 20, the rewards are stickers instead.

## Story

Personal Stories allow players to experience exclusive, visual-novel-style lore chapters centered around the specific Costume.

There are 6 episodes in total, which unlock sequentially as you hit the specific level milestones. You also need to read the previous episode **to unlock** the next one.

!!! abstract "Episode Requirements"

     * Episode 1 — **Level 1**
     * Episode 2 — **Level 3**
     * Episode 3 — **Level 5**
     * Episode 4 — **Level 7**
     * Episode 5 — **Level 9**
     * Episode 6 — **Level 10**

Reading a Story episode **does not grant Rapport EXP** — it only rewards {{Dia}} **Dias**, as detailed below.

Whenever the [Season Event](./events.md) revolves around a specific Costume, their Story is unlocked for free for the duration of the Event regardless of the Costume's presence in your account, additionally giving **Event Currency** on top of 80 {{Dia}} **Dias** for each episode by default. When the Season Event ends, the Story gets locked once again until you meet the requirements to read it like any other Costume's Story. It will be considered read after you re-unlock it.

## Rapport Buff

The Rapport Buff is a buff that increases {{CritRate}} **Crit Rate**{.white} and acts like a **Collection Bonus**.

This means the bonus is **global**, affects **all permanent Companions**, and **cannot be reduced via Pressure Effects**.

It has a maximum value of **50%** and is obtained via completing Missions. Each mission gives 2%.

!!! image "Rapport Buff Missions"
     ![Rapport Buff Missions](../assets/images/rapport/rapport-buff.avif)

!!! abstract "Missions List"

     * Reach Rapport Level 10 for [Costume]
     * Reach Rapport Level 20 for [Costume]
     * Reach Rapport Level 10 for 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 Character(s)
     * Reach Rapport Level 15 for 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 Character(s)
     * Reach Rapport Level 20 for 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 Character(s)

!!! info "In-Game Text Clarification"
     The game's mission text uses the word **"Characters"**, but the system tracks **"Costumes"** instead. It is a bad translation that can be confusing when the Rapport system adds more Costumes.

It takes **10 Costumes** with Rapport Level 20 to obtain the maximum Rapport Buff.
