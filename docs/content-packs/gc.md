**Golden Colosseum** is a weekly seasonal [PvP](../misc/slang.md?term=PvP) content.

!!! tip "TL;DR"
    * A Golden Colosseum season lasts one week, concluding with a brief Settlement period.
    * Golden Colosseum does not use [Gear](../progression/gear.md). Only Costumes upgrade levels (+5), [Potential Liberation](../progression/potentials.md), Awakening, and [Bursts](../progression/burst.md) matter.
    * Completing missions and ranking up rewards {{Aurum_Coin}} **Aurum Coins**, which can be spent in the Golden Colosseum Shop.

!!! image "Golden Colosseum Main Menu"
    ![Golden Colosseum Main Menu](../assets/images/gc/gc.avif)

## Basics

**The Golden Colosseum** is unlocked upon clearing all **Pagan Tower** (Story Pack 6) Main Quests.

To fight, you set up a **team** and **Blessings** tailored to the active season's [Rules](#rules). While core combat mechanics remain stable across most seasons, specific parameters (like grid size, unit limits, and bans) rotate regularly.

Similarly to [Mirror Wars](./mirror-wars.md), the battles are automatic and do not require an active input from a player.

## Team

The team consists of **Costumes, not Characters**. You set up one team for both offense and defense, as well as for going first or second.

Each Costume is separate, which means you can put multiple Costumes of the same Character. Additionally, each Costume is affected by its [**own Costume bond**](../progression/potentials.md#costume-bonds), Awakening, Character Level, and Costume Upgrade level. **The only thing Costumes are not affected by is [Gear](../progression/gear.md)**.

To set the team, press the **Formation** button, then **Replace**, pick the needed Costume, and, finally, place it on the field and save the changes.

??? image "Image Guide"
    ![Setting up Formation](../assets/images/gc/Setup-Costumes.avif)

Additionally, in the **Formation** tab, you can freely change the order of your Costumes, similar to the regular [battles](../mechanics/battle.md#__tabbed_2_2).

## Blessings

Blessings are effects that impact you or your enemy. They are separated into three categories: **Offense**, **Defense**, and **Utility**.

To access the Blessings menu, press the **Formation** button followed by **Settings** on the right.

??? image "Image Guide"
    ![Blessings Settings location](../assets/images/gc/blessings-access.avif)

!!! image "Blessings Menu"
    ![Blessings Menu](../assets/images/gc/blessings.avif)

Each Blessing costs a specific amount of **Blessing Points**, based on how impactful the Blessing is. You can equip a limited number of Blessings, dictated by the total Blessing Points available. You also have to make two separate Blessing builds for going **First** or **Second**, with going second granting extra points to compensate for turn order.

To check each Blessing, refer to the Blessing Menu mentioned above. Alternatively, check [Souseha's Database](https://browndust2-db.souseha.com/en/blessings).

!!! question "What Blessings to choose?"
    It comes down to the used team and the strategy. Check the [Guide](#guide) section below. 

    If you want to use Blessings of the other person but have only a list for going first or second, you can usually adjust it by picking higher/lower potency Blessings for more/less points.
    
    For example, if the person has Curse of Limits I in their "going first" lineup, you can try picking **both** Curse of Limits I and **Curse of Limits II** for when you go second.

### "Consistent" Rules

While parameters labeled as <span class="yellow">(Consistent)</span> can technically be altered by developers in future seasons, they have remained stable defaults throughout 34 seasons:

* **Weekly Entry:** You are given **100 base attempts** per week. You can purchase up to **3 refills** (100 attempts each) for **10 {{Dia}} Dia** per refill, capping out at **400 attempts for 30 {{Dia}} Dia total** per season.
* **Initiative:** You cannot choose whether you go first or second; initiative is decided randomly at the start of battle.
* **Action Flow:** Units alternate turns individually based on placement order.<br>(`Player Costume 1 → Enemy Costume 1 → Player Costume 2 → Enemy Costume 2...`) (if you go first).
    * If the Enemy Costume 1 dies from Player Costume 1, Enemy Costume 2 takes its place, until all deployed enemy Costumes have acted.
    * If the enemy used all their Costumes in a given Turn, but you still have multiple unused Costumes, they will do their actions without the enemy using skills once again.
* **Cooldowns:** Costumes have no Cooldowns between Turns.
* **SP Costs:** SP is set to ∞ (ignored/bypassed), meaning skills do not cost SP to cast. However, available SP for scaling mechanics (e.g., Olivier) is treated as baseline 0.
* **Starting Points:** You start with 1000 Ranking points.
* **Death Time Turn:** At **Turn 5**, combat enters Death Time. Unlike standard game modes where skills remain active during Death Time, skills in Golden Colosseum are **disabled completely**, forcing units to use basic attacks alongside the ramping Death Time stat buffs/debuffs.

To get them, press the corresponding icon in the Golden Colosseum menu.

!!! tip "If you are very busy, ..."
    ...at least win 10 fights per Season. You will lose around 22% of coins from Missions alone and some coins from ranking, but at least it will be better than not participating at all.

    10 wins is not hard if you find a good team for a given season.

!!! tip "Missions Availability"
    You cannot **complete** Missions during the **Settlement Period**.

    However, you still can **claim rewards** during the **Settlement Period**, given you satisfied the conditions beforehand.

    It is useful when you are completing the Colosseum at the last minute or just forget to claim before the period kicks in.
