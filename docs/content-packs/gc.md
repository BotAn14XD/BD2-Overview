**Golden Colosseum** is a weekly seasonal [PvP](../misc/slang.md?term=PvP) content.

!!! tip "TL;DR"
    * A Golden Colosseum season lasts one week, concluding with a brief Settlement period.
    * Golden Colosseum does not use [Gear](../progression/gear.md). Only Costumes upgrades (+5), [Potential Liberation](../progression/potentials.md), Awakening and [Bursts](../progression/burst.md) matter.
    * Completing missions and ranking up rewards {{Aurum_Coin}} **Aurum Coins**, which can be spent in the Golden Colosseum Shop.

## Basics

### Unlock Requirements

**The Golden Colosseum** is unlocked upon clearing all **Pagan Tower** (Story Pack 6) Main Quests.
