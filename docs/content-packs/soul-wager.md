#

**The Soul Wager** is an Auto Battler Inventory Management Content Pack. In it, your goal is to clear a set number of **Boards** (each consisting of several stages) within a limited number of attempts within the same Board.

!!! image "The Soul Wager Screen"
    ![The Soul Wager Screen](../assets/images/soul-wager/soul-wager-menu.avif)

---

## Basics

### Unlock Requirements

**The Soul Wager** is unlocked upon clearing all **Edge of Dimension** Main Quests.

### Overview

The Soul Wager is **seasonal** content, with each season lasting $\bf{8}$ weeks. During the season, you have unlimited attempts to clear all **Boards**, each containing a fixed number of **Phases** (battles). To complete the Board, you must complete all Phases.

To do that, you must purchase **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear** from the **Soul Wager in-battle Shop**, place them on the **Main Field**, and start the **automated** battle.

For any Victory or Defeat, you receive {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens** to continue your run ($10$ {{Soul_Token}} for a loss and $14$ {{Soul_Token}} for a victory).

If you are defeated, the Battle Shop refreshes and the reroll counter resets, giving you a clean slate of options to try on your next attempt. However, if you lose all 5 of your {{ Life_Count }} Attempts (Defeats), the current Board completely resets, sending you back to Phase $1$.

If you successfully complete the Board, you get {{ Alea_Chip }} **Alea Chips** as shop currency to purchase resources for your general progression.

## Field

The Phase Formation screen consists of three sections: **Storage**, **Main Field**, and **Shop**.
!!! image "Formation Screen layout"
    ![Formation Screen Layout](../assets/images/soul-wager/layout.avif)

### Main Field

Main Field is a $7 \times 7$ field where you place your **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear**.

Each tile varies in size, ranging from $1 \times 1$ ($1$ square) to $2 \times 2$ or $1 \times 4$ ($4$ squares).

Units are placed on top of these tiles. Each unit has a unique footprint, varying in size from $1 \times 1$ ($1$ square) to $3 \times 4$ with missing corners ($8$ tiles).

!!! example "Rotation"
    You can **rotate** your Tiles and Units.

    * **PC**: Hold **LMB** and click **RMB** to rotate clock-wise by $90\degree$.
    * **Phone / Tablet:** Tap and hold with one finger, then tap with another to rotate clockwise by $90\degree$.

??? image "Main Field Grid"
    ![Main Field Grid](../assets/images/soul-wager/main-field.avif)

### Enemy Formation (View Formation)

Before or during a run, you can press the **View Formation** button to inspect the enemy's active grid layout and Unit Actions, allowing you to develop a strategy to counter them.

??? image "View Formation Screen"
    ![Image Guide](../assets/images/soul-wager/view-formation.avif)

Using this tool is important for high-level play, as it allows you to build the strategy around enemy weaknesses.

### The Battle Shop

**The Battle Shop** offers you $4$ slots, where **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear** can appear.

After each battle, the Battle Shop is refreshed, regardless of the outcome of the battle.

Each board piece has an equal chance to appear within the **same Grade**; however, the chances of drawing a specific **Grade** heavily depend on **Stage Phase** and **Board**.

* For example, **Tainted Hunting Grounds** *(Board 5)* **Stage Phase 1** can only have **Normal** and **Rare** Grades (with $83.33\%$ and $16.66\%$ respectively), while **Stage Phase 15** offers all $6$ Grades with equal chance of $16.66\%$.

!!! warning "Reserving"
    If you want a board piece to stay in the shop until the next Formation Stage, you can **click on the price tag** to **reserve it**. This way, it will not be refreshed after the next battle.

    To undo the reservation, simply repeat the given action.

    ??? image "Reserving Board Pieces"
        ![Reserving Board Pieces](../assets/images/soul-wager/reservation.avif)

!!! example "Discounts"
    Some deals may feature a **reduced** price.

    * Chance for a discount is $10\%$.
    * The discount is calculated as $\text{round}(0.5 \times \text{Price})$, providing roughly a $50\%$ discount.
    * Calculated prices are always rounded up.
        * For example, **Lathel - Homunculus** with the discount $(0.5 \times 5 = 2.5)$ will cost $\bf{3}$ instead.

!!! abstract "Reroll"
    If you don't like the shop deals, you can **Reroll** the shop, using {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens**.
    
    The Reroll button is located at the bottom right part of the screen.

    ??? image "Reroll Button"
        ![Reroll Button](../assets/images/soul-wager/reroll.avif)

    Rerolling costs $1$ {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens** and and increases by $1$ after every $4$ rolls within the same **Formation Phase**. Therefore, it is not recommended to reroll more than $4-5$ times per Phase.

!!! example "Selling"
    You can sell existing Board Pieces to obtain some {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens** back. To do that, either select a board piece and click **Sell**, or directly **drag** the piece to the Sell button.

    ??? image "Different Selling Options"
        ![Different Selling Options](../assets/images/soul-wager/sell_options.avif)

    * Selling price is calculated as $\text{round}(0.5 \times \text{Purchase Price})$, which means Unit upgrades do not increase the Price.
    * Rounding always is **down**, but the price cannot be less than $2$, with **Protection Tile** being the only exception —  having a selling price of $1$.
        * For example, **Lathel - Homunculus** $(0.5 \times 5 = 2.5)$ will give $\bf{2}$ instead, but Arines - Priest of Vitality $(0.5 \times 3 = 1.5)$ will give $\bf{2}$, compared to $1$ she was supposed to give.

### Storage

Storage is a space where you can leave your purchased Board Pieces. They will not be used in a battle, and there is no penalty for storing them.

??? "Storage Image"
    ![Storage Image](../assets/images/soul-wager/storage.avif)

You can filter your Storage by **Tiles**, **Units**, **Gear** or **All** at the same time.

Additionally, the Storage zone features a "gravity" system. Placing any stored piece on the left side of the storage panel automatically slides it flush against the right boundary, behaving like a sideways Tetris stack.

### Tiles

There are a total of 5 different Tiles, each varying in shape, cost and abilities. They are required to place the Units on.

??? image "Tile Codex"
    ![Tile Codex](../assets/images/soul-wager/gear-codex.avif)


### Units

Units can only be placed on top of the Tiles. To deploy and use a unit, you must have unoccupied tile space available.

Each Unit has different **Grade** and **Tags**. While **Grade** determines a unit's shop appearance rarity, **Tags** briefly outline their gameplay role.

!!! example "Tags List"

    There are a total of $\bf{8}$ Tags:

    * {{Close_Range_Attack}} **Close-Range Attack**: This Character performs a close-range attack upon every cooldown reset.
    * {{Defending}} **Defending**: Defending Characters have abilities that are triggered when the opponent attacks.
    * {{Support}} **Support (Utility)**: Utility Characters grant Buffs to their allies.
    * {{Heal}} **Heal**: Heal Characters have the ability to restore HP.
    * {{Curse}} **Curse**: Curse Characters apply Debuffs to the opponent.
    * {{Long_Range_Attack}} **Long-Range Attack**: This Character performs a long-range attack upon every cooldown reset.
    * {{Cleanse}} **Cleanse**: Cleanse Characters remove Debuffs on yourself.
    * {{Remove}} **Remove**: Remove Characters remove Buffs on the opponent.

Every **attacking** unit has a few key stats:

* **DMG** — Damage per hit. It features its own value range, with a random value selected for each Action.
* **Stamina Cost** — Stamina used per Action.
* **Accuracy Rate** — The chance that an Action successfully deals damage to the enemy.
* **Cooldown** — The interval between a Unit's Actions.

!!! image "Unit Description Explanation"
    ![Unit Description Explanation](../assets/images/soul-wager/codex_explanation.avif)

Certain units benefit from specific conditions tied to how you place their tiles on the grid. These requirements are indicated on their skill cards by the {{Cross}} icon. These bonuses only trigger if the required tiles are actually placed on the battlefield grid.

For example, you cannot trigger **Elise - Code Name O**'s enhanced restoration if her target {{Cross}} tiles are rotated off the active board layout.

??? image "Image Explanation"
    ![Image Explanation](../assets/images/soul-wager/cross-bonus.avif)

Additionally, certain Units generate **Buffs** for the team or **Debuffs** for the enemy. You can learn more about them from Units' cards or check below.

!!! example "Soul Wager Buffs"

    * {{Regeneration}} | {{Regeneration2}} **Regeneration**: For every stack, restores $1$ HP every $2$s.
    * {{Overheat}} | {{Overheat2}} **Overheat**: For every stack, increases Action Speed by $2\%$.
    * {{Lifesteal}} | {{Lifesteal2}} **Lifesteal**: For every stack, restores HP equal to close-range DMG dealt. Healing is capped at max stacks.
    * {{Focus}} | {{Focus2}} **Focus**: For every stack, increases Accuracy Rate of all Characters by $5\%$.
    * {{SP}} | {{SP2}} **SP**: Used to trigger certain effects. SP in and of itself does not have effects.
    * {{Strength}} | {{Strength2}} **Strength**: For every stack, increases the DMG dealt by Attacking Characters by 1.
    * {{Counter}} | {{Counter2}} **Counter**: For every stack, deals Counter DMG equal to DMG taken from close-range attacks. If DMG is higher than stack count, deals Counter DMG equal to stack count.

!!! example "Soul Wager Debuffs"

    * {{Poison}} | {{Poison2}} **Poison**: For every stack, takes $1$ DMG every $2$s.
    * {{Frost}} | {{Frost2}} **Frost**: For every stack, reduces Action Speed of all Characters by $2\%$
    * {{Darkness}} | {{Darkness2}} **Darkness**: For every stack, reduces Accuracy Rate of all Characters by $5\%$.
    * {{Laceration}} | {{Laceration2}} **Laceration**: For every stack, increases Stamina Cost of Attacking Characters by $2\%$.

!!! abstract "Units Upgrading"

    You can **upgrade the Units**, which will improve their Action. Units can be upgraded to $+5$, similarly to the main game, with the exception that you **cannot upgrade a character higher than he is in your in-game roster.**

    Each Upgrade level costs differently for each Unit.

## Battle

Pressing the "Battle" button in the bottom right corner begins the match.

!!! image "Battle Screen"
    ![Battle Screen](../assets/images/soul-wager/battle.avif)

Combat is fully automated and continues until one side's total $\text{HP}$ is reduced to $0$. If both sides reach $0\text{ HP}$ simultaneously, the player is declared the winner.

During the battle, all Units on the field perform their actions based on their respective cooldowns. The only exceptions are Units that lack individual cooldowns, instead triggering off incoming **enemy attacks**, **allied actions**, **specific conditional actions**, or **"Upon Battle Start" triggers** *(such as <b>Seir - Demon's Daughter</b>, <b>Eclipse - Dream Bride</b>, <b>Refithea - Poolside Fairy</b>, and <b>Jayden - Beautiful Girl Devotee</b>, respectively)*.

While grid placement matters for triggering team synergy bonuses, spatial positioning does not affect combat targeting. All deployed units strike the entire enemy field simultaneously, meaning you can place your DPS units on any tile or rotate the board layout without changing your team's total damage output.

When a unit performs an Action that requires **Stamina**, the cost specified on its Unit card is deducted from the team's shared Stamina pool. **If the shared pool lacks enough Stamina to fund the action, the Unit cannot perform the move** *(displaying "Out of Stamina")* and will enter its cooldown cycle once again.

### End Game

If a match takes longer than 20 seconds to conclude, the **End Game** mechanic activates. This mechanic inflicts damage over time (DoT) every second to both sides, based on the value displayed under the **END GAME label**.

The damage scales upward every 3 seconds according to the following logic:

* **Below 10 Damage:** The value increases by a flat $+1$.
* **10 Damage and Above:** The value begins scaling by $11\%$ every $3$ seconds.

??? info "Exact Scaling Formula"
    In reality, the system runs an exponential $11\%$ scaling curve in the background starting from second 1. To smoothen the damage curve from the beginning, the system uses $+1$ increments instead.

    Once the background exponential curve naturally surpasses 10, the game switches from the flat $+1$ floor to the true calculated curve. Because the background curve does not land exactly on 10 before its next iteration, subsequent values are derived from the underlying unrounded formula rather than exactly $10 \times 1.11$. All final ticking damage values are rounded up to the nearest integer.

    $$
    \text{Damage} = \left\lfloor\frac{\text{Current Battle Time}}{3}\right\rfloor - 6, \: 20 \le \text{Current Battle Time} \le 48 \\
    \text{Damage} = \left\lceil 1.11^{\left\lfloor\frac{\text{Current Battle Time}}{3}\right\rfloor + 6}\right\rceil, \text{Current Battle Time} \ge 48
    $$

## Strategy

Boards $1 — 3$ don't require specific strategy, since enemy isn't that strong. As long as you build decent synergy within the team, it should work well.

When it comes to Boards $4 — 5$, the easiest strategy revolves around using $1-2$ **Ingrid - Kardis' Bullet** and **Lathel - Homunculus** to buff Ingrid.

The goal is to burst down the enemy before passive effects like {{Lifesteal2}} **Lifesteal** or {{Poison2}} **Poison** can influence the match, thanks to Ingrid's very low cooldown and double attack each Cooldown.

Since **Lathel - Homunculus** has **Rare** Grade, and **Ingrid - Kardis' Bullet** has **Basic** Grade, you can theoretically get both from the start of your run.

Other good support units for this strategy are **Blade - Apostle**, **Arines - Priest of Vitality**, **Sonya - Pumpkin Ghost** and **Elise - Code Name O**.
