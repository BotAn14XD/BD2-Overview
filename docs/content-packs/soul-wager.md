#

**The Soul Wager** is an Auto Battler Inventory Management Content Pack. In it, your goal is to clear a set amount of **Boards** that consist of stages, in a given amount of attempts.

!!! image "The Soul Wager Screen"
    ![The Soul Wager Screen](../assets/images/soul-wager/soul-wager-menu.avif)

## Basics

### Unlock Requirements

**The Soul Wager** is unlocked upon clearing all **Edge of Dimension** Main Quests.

### Overview

The Soul Wager is a **seasonal** content, each season lasting $\bf{8}$ weeks. During the season, you have unlimited attempts to clear all the **Boards**, containing fixed amount of **Phases** (Battles). To complete the Board, you must complete all Phases.

To do that, you need to buy **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear** from the **Soul Wager in-battle Shop**, place them inside the **Main Field** and start the **automatic** battle.

For any Victory or Defeat, you get {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens** to continue your run. If you use all your 5 {{ Life_Count }} Attempts (Defeats), the <u>**current**</u> Board **resets**, sending you to the Phase $1$ again.

If you successfully complete the Board, you get {{ Alea_Chip }} **Alea Chips** as a shop currency to buy resources for your general progression.

## Field

The Phase Formation screen contains from three parts: **Storage**, **Main Field**, and **Shop**.
!!! image "Formation Screen layout"
    ![Formation Screen Layout](../assets/images/soul-wager/layout.avif)

### Main Field

Main Field is a $7 \times 7$ field where you place your **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear**.

Each tile varies from $1 \times 1$ ($1$ square) to $2 \times 2$ or $1 \times 4$ ($4$ squares)

On top of each Tile, you can put the Units. Each Unit occupies different space, varying from $1 \times 1$ ($1$ square) to $3 \times 4$ with missing corners ($8$ tiles).

!!! example "Rotation"
    You can **rotate** your Tiles and Units.

    * **PC**: Hold **LMB** and click **RMB** to rotate clock-wise by $90\degree$.
    * **Phone / Tablet:** Hold with one finger and click with another to rotate clock-wise by $90\degree$.

??? image "Main Field Grid"
    ![Main Field Grid](../assets/images/soul-wager/main-field.avif)

### The Battle Shop

**The Battle Shop** offers you $4$ slots, where **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear** can appear.

After each battle, the Battle Shop is refreshed, regardless of the outcome of the battle.

Each Board Piece has equal chance to appear within **same Grade**, however, chances for specific **Grade** heavily depend on **Stage Phase** and **Board**.

* For example, **Tainted Hunting Grounds** *(Board 5)* **Stage Phase 1** can only have **Normal** and **Rare** Grades (with $83.33\%$ and $16.66\%$ respectively), while **Stage Phase 15** offers all $6$ Grades with equal chance of $16.66\%$.

!!! warning "Reserving"
    If you want the Board Piece to stay in the shop until the next Formation Stage, you can **click on the price tag** to **reserve it**. This way, it will not be refreshed after the next battle.

    To undo the reservation, simply repeat the given action.

    ??? image "Reserving Board Pieces"
        ![Reserving Board Pieces](../assets/images/soul-wager/reservation.avif)

!!! example "Discounts"
    Some deals can have **reduced** price.

    * Chance for a discount is $10\%$.
    * Discount is calculated as $\text{round}(0.5 \times \text{Price})$, providing roughly $50\%$ discount.
    * Price rounding is always up.
        * For example, **Lathel - Homunculus** with the discount $(0.5 \times 5 = 2.5)$ will cost $\bf{3}$ instead.

!!! abstract "Reroll"
    If you don't like the shop deals, you can **Reroll** the shop, using {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens**.
    
    The Reroll button is located at the bottom right part of the screen.

    ??? image "Reroll Button"
        ![Reroll Button](../assets/images/soul-wager/reroll.avif)

    Reroll costs $1$ {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens** and increases by $1$ after each $4$ rolls **within same Formation Phase**. Therefore, it is not recommended to reroll more than $4-5$ times per Phase.

!!! example "Selling"
    You can sell existing Board Pieces to obtain some {{ Soul_Token }} **Soul Tokens** back. To do that, either press on the Board Piece and then click the **Sell**, or directly **drag** the Board Piece to the sell button. 

    ??? image "Different Selling Options"
        ![Different Selling Options](../assets/images/soul-wager/sell_options.avif)

    * Selling price is calculated as $\text{round}(0.5 \times \text{Purchase Price})$, which means Unit upgrades do not increase the Price.
    * Rounding always is **down**, but the price cannot be less than $2$. 
        * For example, **Lathel - Homunculus** $(0.5 \times 5 = 2.5)$ will give $\bf{2}$ instead, but Arines - Priest of Vitality $(0.5 \times 3 = 1.5)$ will give $\bf{2}$, compared to $1$ she was supposed to give.

### Storage

The Storage is a place where you can leave your purchased Board Pieces. They will not be used in a battle, and there is no penalty for storing them.

??? "Storage Image"
    ![Storage Image](../assets/images/soul-wager/storage.avif)

You can filter your Storage by **Tiles**, **Units**, **Gear** or **All** at the same time.

Additionally, Storage has a "gravity" feature that pulls everything towards the right part of the storage, behaving like sideways Tetris blocks.

The **Codex** is a place to learn more about **Tiles**, **Units** and **Gear**. To access it, you can press the corresponding icon on top of the screen pre-run, or, during the run, on the bottom of your screen.

### Tiles

There a total of 5 different Tiles, each varying in shape, cost and abilities. 

### Units

Each Unit has different **Grade** and **Tags**. While **Grade** serves as Shop appearance divider, **Tags** briefly explain what each Unit does.

There a total of $\bf{8}$ Tags:

* {{Close_Range_Attack}} **Close-Range Attack**: This Character performs a close-range attack upon every cooldown reset.
* {{Defending}} **Defending**: Defending Characters have abilities that are triggered when the opponent attacks.
* {{Support}} **Support (Utility)**: Utility Characters grant Buffs to their allies.
* {{Heal}} **Heal**: Heal Characters have the ability to restore HP.
* {{Curse}} **Curse**: Curse Characters apply Debuffs to the opponent.
* {{Long_Range_Attack}} **Long-Range Attack**: This Character performs a long-range attack upon every cooldown reset.
* {{Cleanse}} **Cleanse**: Cleanse Characters remove Debuffs on yourself.
* {{Remove}} **Remove**: Remove Characters remove Buffs on the opponent.
