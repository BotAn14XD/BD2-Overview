    Gacha, Summons & Economy¶

    Is there a Tier List?

    This game is roster-based, not team-based; thus there is no reliable Tier List since you tend to use different characters depending on the situation. Anything you see online can have some degree of truth, but it still wouldn't capture the whole complexity of the game.

    The only thing that stays correct all the time:
    Supports are the highest priority since you use them constantly, unlike DPS.

    How do I get Characters? How does the costume system work?

    You can think of Costumes as Skills of a Character.

    Whenever you obtain the first costume of the Character, they are unlocked automatically. You do not need to pull for Characters.

    Who should I pull for?

    Refer to Banner Recommendations.

    Alternatively, as a new player, you can spend some amount of pulls into 12-Pick Selective Banner, prioritizing Supports.

    View 12-Pick Recommendations →

    Who do I get from the Newbie Infinite Draw?

    The best Costume to get from there is The Curse Celia.

    She is a late-game oriented pick, so you should not expect immediate benefit from her. Nonetheless, she is an important Chainer to have.

    Do not worry about any weapons or other costumes; it is not worth the time spent on rerolling the banner.

    How does the Selective Draw work? Is 1-Pick or 12-Pick better?

    Selective Draw (Banners) work in a way that you pick desired Costumes and they have increased chances to appear. They are the only banners that accept Selective Draw Tickets.

    Essentially, Selective Draw is displayed in the form of 12-Pick (Permanent) and 1-Pick (recurring) Banners.

    As a new player, 12-Pick is objectively better.

    Who to pick from Recommended ★5 Costume Selective Ticket?

    Generally, it depends on who you have from those tickets beforehand.

    Assuming you have no one, you should pick

    • Adventurer of the Unknown Diana +2 (3 tickets)
    • B-Rank Idol Helena +1 (2 tickets)
    • Homunculus Lathel +2 (3 tickets)
    • New Hire Seir +0 (1 ticket)
    • Track and Field Captain Levia +0 (1 ticket)
    • Robin Hood Zenith +0 (1 ticket)
    • Game Club Rafina +0 (1 ticket)

    Assuming you also bought Mega SALE!!! First Time Countdown Package!, your choice changes to:

    • Adventurer of the Unknown Diana +4 (5 tickets)
    • B-Rank Idol Helena +3 (4 tickets)
    • Homunculus Lathel +3 (4 tickets)
    • ...

    Order, generally speaking, does not matter since at the end you will have the same costume set either way. Nonetheless, you can start by picking B-Rank Idol Helena +1 first to comfortably run your starting team.

    If you are not sure, it is always better to ask in a Discord Server.

    How do Property Tickets work? Who do I pick?

    Property Selective Draw Exchange Ticket allows you to choose a Property and receive a random ★5 Costume from the chosen Property.

    As a new player, almost every Property is good to spend tickets on. You can focus on Light until you get decent Refithea.
    Later on, you can do the math to see what gives more useful Costumes on average.
    Even later on, you can do the math to see what gives less Threads on average due to owning a lot of +5 Costumes.

    Battle & Mechanics¶

    What is 'Property' and how does it work?

    Property is technically the Element of each Character.

    There are a lotal of 6 Properties: Water, Fire, Wind, Light, Darkness and Neutral.

    • Water Allies deal more damage to Fire Enemies and less damage to Wind Enemies
    • Fire Allies deal more damage to Wind Enemies and less damage to Water Enemies
    • Wind Allies deal more damage to Water Enemies and less damage to Fire Enemies
    • Light Allies deal more damage to Darkness Enemies
    • Darkness Allies deal more damage to Light Enemies

    Neutral Property cannot receive more or less damage from any other Property.

    Increased damage can be influenced by Property Buffs.

    How to build a good team?

    A good (general content) team:

    • Uses only one type of damage, Physical or Magical (unless you absolutely know what you are doing and can explain every single moment regarding your decision)
    • Uses both Supports (usually 3, can be two) and DPS
    • Has a correct order of Costumes (Supports first, DPS second)
    • Does NOT use Property Buffs whenever the team is at no gain from it
    • Focuses either on Large AoE Damage (for regular fights) or Single Target Nuke Damage (for Solo Boss fights)
    • Uses different Buff Types whenever it is possible (Augmentation / Vulnerability, ATK Buff etc).
    • Is built around finishing the battle on Turn 1, or, at max, on Turn 3
    • Can activate all abilities on Turn 1, or, in other words, has enough SP
    • Has the best Gear you have at the moment

    Upgrades & Progression¶

    Who do I level/upgrade first?

    You need to be careful when using the upgrade term, since it can be applied to a lot of Costumes' / Characters' aspects.

    • When it comes down to Level, prefer using Slimes for your DPS
    • When it comes down to Potential Liberation, focus on Supports first.
      More about Potential Liberation →
    • When it comes down to Gear, prioritize DPS.
      More about Gear System →
    • When it comes down to Engraving / Awakening, it is used on DPS, but it is very expensive for a new player, so hold onto resources.
      • If you do this for Guide Pass Task, Awakening Nebris is one of the best choices
    • When it comes down to Burst, there is no definitive answer since the mechanic is new. However, the Author suggests focusing on Supports, similar to Potential Liberation
      More about Burst System →
    • When it comes down to Costume copies, generally Supports are preferred; however, there may be exceptions. Refer to Banner Recommendations in case you are hesitating.

    Should I Engrave / Awaken someone? Who to Engrave / Awaken?

    As was stated in the "Who do I level/upgrade first?" question, engraving is not worth it for new players. This is an expensive procedure that does not give much benefit.

    Nonetheless, if you decided to Engrave / Awaken someone, it should be the DPS you use the most, such as Nebris.

    Technical & Community¶

    How can I play the game on PC?

    You can download the client using this link.

    My characters appear to be black(ened). How do I fix it?

    This happens because Silhouette Mode is enabled. It uses silhouettes instead of sprites and disables animations.

    To remove it, head to Settings → Graphics → Silhouette Mode → Disable.

    Image Guide

    Is there censorship in this game?
    • One active form of censorship is for German citizens. There, a few game sprites have altered appearance, as well as some Ability cutscenes, Medical Club Teresse Fated Guest, and Slap Pop Pop! Minigame were removed.
    • Another active form of censorship is for Korean citizens, where Shattered Dream Palette and Retired Legend Olivier cutscenes were modified, as well as Retired Legend Olivier Fated Guest was modified as well.
    • The game is not legally accessible in Vietnam and Netherlands, but there are workarounds to download the game from there.
    How to display my own collection when asking for a team build?
    • When asking for a team, use Last Night to show the Costumes you have. To do that,
      1. Go to the Last Night Pack and walk towards the battle area
      2. Tap Replace → Replace → Reset → Confirm.
      3. Click the second icon in the top right corner and sort "By Deployed First ↓" and screenshot your costumes.
